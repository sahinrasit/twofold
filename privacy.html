Privacy Policy

Last updated: 12 October 2026

Twofold is a reader for PDFs, EPUB books and comics. It is designed so that your documents and notes never leave your device.

Your documents and notes

Files you open in Twofold are copied into the app's private storage on your device. Highlights, notes, bookmarks and drawings are stored on your device as well. We do not upload, read, analyse or back up your documents or notes, and Twofold has no account system. If you delete the app, this data is deleted with it, except for any copies you exported yourself.

Purchases

Twofold Pro is a one-time in-app purchase processed by Apple. We never see your name, Apple Account or payment details. To confirm that a purchase is valid and to restore it on your other devices, the app uses RevenueCat, a purchase-management service. RevenueCat receives a random identifier generated by the app, your purchase receipt from Apple, the app version, device model and country or region. This information is used only to provide and restore your purchase. See RevenueCat's privacy policy.

What we do not collect

  • No analytics or advertising SDKs
  • No tracking across apps or websites
  • No contacts, location, photos or microphone access
  • No sale or sharing of personal data

Children

Twofold does not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date above.
