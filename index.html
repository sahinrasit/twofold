Twofold

Open it. Your desk is ready.

A reader for PDFs, EPUBs and comics built for a phone that opens like a book — your document on the left page, your notes on the right.

Features

  • Page + Notes, Two Pages and Tabletop layouts that keep your place as you fold, unfold or rotate.
  • PDF with search and contents, EPUB with themes, CBZ comics with spreads and right-to-left manga.
  • Highlight in four colors, underline, strike through and draw — saved into your PDF.
  • Export the annotated PDF or your notes as Markdown.
  • No account, nothing uploaded, works offline.

Help & FAQ

How do I add documents?

Tap + in your library to pick PDFs, EPUBs or CBZ files from Files or iCloud Drive, or use Share → Twofold from Mail, Safari or any other app.

When do I see two pages?

Whenever the screen is wide enough: an unfolded foldable iPhone, an iPad, or an iPhone in landscape. Choose Page + Notes, Two Pages or Tabletop from the reader's ⋯ menu.

How do I highlight?

Press and hold text, drag to select, then tap a color in the bar that appears. Highlights appear in the notes page with the quoted text.

What does Twofold Pro include?

Unlimited documents, freehand ink, export and the Tabletop layout, for a single one-time payment. Family Sharing is supported. The free version keeps three of your own documents.

I reinstalled the app. How do I get Pro back?

Open Settings → Restore Purchases while signed in with the same Apple Account.

Where are my files stored?

Only on your device, inside Twofold. Export a document or your notes any time from the reader's ⋯ menu.
